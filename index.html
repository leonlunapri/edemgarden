A FAMILY TAVERNA

Welcome to Edem Garden

Edem Garden is a family taverna in Olympos, Karpathos, offering fresh traditional homemade food in a peaceful terrace surrounded by flowers, plants and mountain views.

Tula is the owner of the restaurant and her daughter Popi welcomes guests in the dining room and terrace. Here, traditional recipes, local ingredients and a relaxed atmosphere come together to create a genuine Karpathian experience.

THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

Edem Garden

The restaurant is in a beautiful terrace surrounded by flowers and plants, offering a breathtaking view of the mountains and the traditional village of Olympos. It is a peaceful place to enjoy a meal, a breakfast or simply relax with a greek coffee and take in the scenery.

Come for the food. Stay for the view, the terrace and the warm family atmosphere.

FROM OUR KITCHEN

A Taste of Our Menu

Here is a selection of our most loved dishes — but our kitchen has much more to offer, from fine local wines to creamy Greek yogurt and honey from local producers.

Makarounes

Handmade pasta, a Karpathian speciality, tossed with caramelized onions and local cheese.

Moussaka

Layers of potato, eggplant and minced meat, baked with a creamy béchamel topping.

Souvlaki

Grilled skewers of marinated meat, served with fresh bread and tzatziki.

Greek Salad

Ripe tomatoes, cucumber, olives and feta, dressed simply with olive oil and oregano.

Stuffed Aubergines

Eggplants baked with tomato, herbs and cheese, a rustic island favourite.

Stuffed Tomatoes & Peppers

Tomatoes and peppers filled with rice, herbs and a touch of mint, oven-baked.

Zucchini Flowers

Zucchini flowers filled with rice and herbs, a delicate homemade speciality.

Loukoumades

Warm honey puffs, dusted with cinnamon, a traditional homemade dessert.

Baklava

Layers of crisp filo pastry, walnuts and honey syrup, a classic Greek sweet.
